The research posts on this site were written a few years ago, when I was earlier in my career and wanted somewhere to show work that was not easy to show otherwise. They still hold up, but they are long, and long is a bad format for most of what I want to say now.

This section is for shorter pieces. Three kinds, roughly:

- **Market notes.** What the day-ahead auction or the intraday book did, why, and what it means for anyone running a strategy against it. Written quickly, dated, and left as they were.
- **Notebooks.** A model, a loss function, a backtest pitfall, worked through in code. Short enough to read in a coffee break.
- **Essays.** Occasional longer pieces on where AI is actually useful in energy, and where it is being oversold.

Each piece is a Markdown file with a few lines of front matter. That is the whole publishing workflow, which is the point: the easier it is to post, the more often it happens.
