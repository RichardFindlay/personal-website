About

Richard Findlay

Principal data scientist building algorithmic trading strategies for day-ahead and intraday power markets, with a decade spent on the asset side of the energy industry first.

Now

Leading the research and build of trading algorithms for short-term power markets: signal research, probabilistic forecasting, backtesting, execution logic and the monitoring that keeps strategies honest once they are live.

I came into energy in 2017 through renewables development: early-stage wind farm and co-located storage projects, where the questions were about yield, siting, storage sizing and whether a site was worth the next stage of spend. That is where I learned that most of the value in energy data work is in getting the physics and the market mechanics right before reaching for a model.

From there I moved into applied machine learning, taking computer vision systems from prototype into production for the sector, and then into the markets, where the forecasting and optimisation work I had been doing on batteries and wind became the raw material for trading strategies.

Alongside the day job I build things: ohmera.ai, a GB power-market intelligence product, and a couple of products that have nothing to do with energy at all. This site is where I write about AI in energy, what the market is doing, and what has actually worked.
